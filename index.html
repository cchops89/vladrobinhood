How to buy

THREE STEPS.

01

Download Robinhood Wallet

The self-custody one — “Robinhood Wallet: Swap Crypto” on the App Store or Google Play. Robinhood Chain is built in. Write your recovery phrase down on paper.

02

Fund it with ETH

You need ETH on Robinhood Chain — it's the gas token and what you'll swap from. Buy in-app through Robinhood Connect, send from your Robinhood Crypto account, or bridge it over.

03

Home → Swap → paste the CA

Pick Robinhood Chain on both sides. Put the $VLAD contract in the To field — check it against the lime bar up top, never search by name. Review the quote, swipe to swap.
